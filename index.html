Weekly Workflow

  1. Check MPA standings. Open the MPA sport pages (football seeding, soccer, field hockey and volleyball; check XC meet qualification separately) and copy the latest standings.
  2. Review results. Check school athletics pages, MPA results and trustworthy score reports. Confirm dates, scores and duplicate entries. Keep disagreements pending until resolved.
  3. Re-tag playoff status. Record a dated official rank within its sport, class and region. Label current playoff position separately from a clinched berth.
  4. Note key matchups. Add the next week's biggest games for the nine coverage schools.
  5. Approve and publish. Review the candidate file, update the reviewed data and dated standings, then run the validation and publisher. Newly fetched results remain unpublished until reviewed.
Sources to check each week: mpa.cc · downtownme.com · MaxPreps · school athletics pages.
